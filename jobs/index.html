Please read before applying

Gamerev Games is a self-funded indie studio, and we can't pay salaries, hourly wages, or fees. Every role on our team is an unpaid, profit-sharing position: contributors receive an agreed share of a project's profits once it earns money.

Games don't always make money, so a profit share is never guaranteed income. If you need to be paid for your work, this isn't the right fit right now, and we completely understand.

How profit sharing works

1

Profit share, not salary

There's no hourly pay or upfront payment. Instead, contributors earn a share of the profits from the game they help build.

2

Terms in writing

The share depends on the role and how much you contribute. We agree on it openly, in writing, before any work begins.

3

Credit and portfolio

You're credited in the game and on our site, and you can show the finished work in your portfolio.
