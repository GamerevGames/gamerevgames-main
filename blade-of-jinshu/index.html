Game info

Betrayed and left to die, you rise as a cybernetic-enhanced warrior on a relentless path of revenge. Slash your way through corporate enforcers with fast, fluid combat, overcome deadly challenges, and upgrade your abilities along the way.

Master a deadly arsenal of cyber-enhanced attacks: single blade, dual daggers, and devastating special abilities. Every strike is precise. Every move is lethal. Fight through woodlands, neon-lit city streets, high-tech facilities, and corporate towers. Face cyber challenges where you confront the delusions of your past, and battle corporate enforcers, drones, and cybernetic assassins. Upgrade your weapons and unlock new skills as you go.

Are you ready to slice your path through the heart of the corruption?
