Game info

A third-person action souls-like set in a corrupted, ruined Earth. You are a surgically augmented survivor, resisting a regime that treats human lives as raw material.

When your sister is taken for a next-generation experiment, you fight through mechanical beasts, mutants, and engineered monsters to bring her home. Expect brutal combat, precise controls, and a focused narrative set inside a megastructure built on failed experiments and eugenics.

Your path leads to the Purity Collective, and a choice between survival and sacrifice.
